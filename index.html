How the assets are made

  • Every mesh is generated by the author's own Python toolchain for Blender, from typical dimensions of public standards (138 kV substations, 13.8 kV and low-voltage distribution). Models are generic, not a manufacturer's product.
  • Textures are procedural and tileable (1 m per repetition), with albedo, normal and metallic/smoothness maps.
  • Each piece passes an automatic geometry check (scale, normals, penetrations, operable-part travel) before it is accepted, and each package is imported into empty URP and Built-in projects before release.
  • Claude (Anthropic) was used as a coding assistant for parts of the toolchain and the C# scripts, under the author's direction and review. No generative AI was used for meshes, textures or images.

About

Arkeh_3D is the publisher name of Bruno Goyatá, who builds digital twins of electrical substations for training in VR and on PC. The kits come from that work: the same equipment types, the same real-scale rules, made generic for the Asset Store.
